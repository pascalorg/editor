# Dormer style thumbnails

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool from actual editor screenshots of each dormer on a roof. The existing dormer icon supplied the lavender clay style; the final Dutch thumbnail used the generated hip icon as its style reference. Gambrel and Dutch were regenerated to preserve their distinct roof silhouettes.

Delivered as 256 × 256 transparent WebP, converted with Sharp (quality 90, alpha quality 100).

## gable

Asset: `apps/editor/public/icons/dormer/gable.webp`

Geometry reference: `/tmp/dormer-gable-reference.png`

Final prompt:

Use case: style-transfer. Generate one square building-editor style thumbnail. Image 1 is an actual gable dormer placed on a roof: use the dormer itself as the geometry reference, including its gable roof profile, projecting eave trim, upright walls, single four-pane front window and sill. Image 2 is the existing lavender dormer icon: match its clean soft-lit clay render, palette, framing and isometric three-quarter angle. Isolate only the small dormer, not the large host roof or building. Center the complete dormer at 80% of the image with transparent background. Keep the distinctive gable roof shape from screenshot, simplify the bottom to a clean cutout edge as in image 2. Lavender walls and roof, subtly darker lavender glazing. No UI, text, outlines, roof scene, pedestal, watermark or extra objects.

## dutch

Asset: `apps/editor/public/icons/dormer/dutch.webp`

Geometry reference: `/tmp/dormer-dutch-reference.png`

Final prompt:

Create a transparent lavender clay icon of the DUTCH dormer in image 1. The roof MUST have a broad LOWER HIPPED SKIRT on all four sides, with a much SMALLER UPPER GABLE roof rising out of the middle. At the front, there is a sloping trapezoidal hip surface ABOVE the window and BELOW the small triangular gablet. The upper gable is recessed significantly behind the front wall, not aligned flush with it. Preserve this two-stage compound roof silhouette exactly as shown in image 1. Square centered product icon, soft lavender clay, 80% framing, transparent background. No host roof, UI, text, pedestal. Do not produce a simple triangular gable roof.
